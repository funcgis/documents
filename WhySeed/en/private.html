Privacy Policy

Applies to Haoqi / 好奇搭子 (engineering name WhySeed, Bundle ID com.funcgis.WhySeed).

1. Who we are

Haoqi (好奇搭子) is a parenting conversation coach. Answers are spoken by parents to children. The app is not designed for children to use on their own, and children are not asked to create accounts.

2. Information we collect

By default, diaries, settings, conversation streaks, letters, and repair notes stay on your device.

  • Voice input: the system processes microphone audio for speech recognition;
  • Bedtime or birthday reminders: notification permission status is stored;
  • Purchases: Apple processes payment; we only learn entitlement results, not card numbers;
  • Cloud “I said it wrong” list: an anonymous on-device identifier (not your name or phone number) associates your repair posts and likes.

No account is required. We do not sell your diary or questions, and we do not build advertising profiles from them.

3. Cloud AI and TTS

When you submit a question that is not in the on-device sensitive set, the question text may be sent to the Haoqi backend and processed by Volcengine Doubao (Ark) to generate parent-facing talking points. TTS playback and repair suggestions may also send related text through the same backend.

Sensitive topics stay on-device by default. No request is made if you are offline or do not use these features.

4. How we use information

We use this information only to provide scripts, diary, notifications, membership, and repair features. We do not use it for ad tracking and do not sell it to third parties.

5. Permissions

  • Microphone / speech recognition — only when you tap voice input or recording.
  • Notifications — only after you enable bedtime topics or birthday capsule reminders.

You can revoke permissions anytime in system settings.

6. Children and sensitive topics

The app serves parents. Content about death, body, money, or family conflict is meant to help parents talk carefully; we do not collect data directly from children.

7. Third parties

Text you actively submit may be processed by our backend and Volcengine Doubao services. Payments are handled by Apple. Partners may process data only as needed to provide the service.

8. Your choices

You can delete diary and repair notes in the app, disable notifications, or uninstall the app to clear local data. For more help, see Support or contact us below.
