1. About the app

ShelfSpan helps you track expiry and warranty dates for medicine, food, cosmetics, daily goods, electronics, and more—with local notifications and widgets.

No account is required. Your item list is stored on your device by default.

2. Getting started

  1. Open the app and tap + to add an item.
  2. Choose a category and enter the name and dates (or use barcode scan / photo OCR to help).
  3. Adjust reminder offsets per category in Reminder settings.
  4. Add Home Screen or Lock Screen widgets to see what is expiring soon.

3. Features

Categories

Medicine, Food, Cosmetics, Daily Goods, Digital (electronics), and Other. Each category has tailored date fields and default reminder rules (for example, cosmetics support period-after-opening; digital focuses on warranty).

Barcode scanning

Scan a package barcode to look up product details. Networked lookup is a Pro feature and may be subject to quota limits. If nothing is found, enter details manually or use OCR.

Photo OCR

Photograph production dates, shelf life, or “use by” text to help fill the form. Always verify results before saving.

Local reminders

Local notifications fire before expiry according to your offsets. Enable Notifications in iOS Settings. If you save an item after the usual reminder time (e.g. 09:00), a catch-up alert may arrive shortly afterward.

Widgets

Widgets show upcoming expiries, category lists, and high-risk medicine on the Lock Screen. Data is shared on-device via an App Group.

Backup

Pro users can export or import JSON / Excel (optional photos) for device migration.

4. Pro

The free tier includes manual entry and basic reminders. Pro unlocks:

  • Networked barcode product lookup
  • Photo OCR
  • Custom reminder rules
  • Backup export / import

Yearly subscription and lifetime purchase are available (prices shown in-app / App Store). Purchases and Restore Purchases use your Apple ID.

Restore Purchases only restores entitlements already bought with the current Apple ID. If you have never purchased, “nothing to restore” is expected.

5. Permissions

  • Camera — scan barcodes and capture labels or photos.
  • Photos — attach images from your library.
  • Notifications — deliver expiry reminders.

You can revoke access anytime in Settings. Turning a permission off disables that feature only.

6. FAQ

I didn’t get today’s reminder

  • Confirm Notifications are allowed for the app.
  • Check that the category’s reminders are enabled and include day 0 (on the day).
  • If you edited the item after the scheduled morning time, a catch-up notification may arrive later.

Barcode lookup finds nothing

  • Many products are missing from public databases—enter details manually or use OCR.
  • If your Pro lookup quota is exhausted, wait for the next quota period or use manual / OCR entry.

OCR is inaccurate

Poor lighting, glare, or tiny print reduce accuracy. Shoot the date area clearly and verify before saving.

Widget looks stale

Open the main app once, then refresh the Home Screen. iOS refreshes widgets on its own schedule—not every second.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes on-device items and settings. If you exported backups, delete those files separately wherever you stored them.
