1. About the app

FreshQi helps you track expiry and warranty dates for medicine, food, cosmetics, daily goods, electronics, documents & contracts, and more—with local notifications and widgets.

No account is required. The main shell has five entries: Home · Archive · Add (center +) · Data · Settings. Your item list stays on your device by default.

2. Getting started

  1. Open the app and tap the green + in the center of the tab bar to add an item.
  2. Choose a category and enter the name and dates. With Pro, you can use barcode scan or photo recognition to help.
  3. After saving, the app schedules built-in local reminders. Customizing reminder offsets requires Pro (Settings → Notification Settings).
  4. When finished, swipe left on Home to archive; visit the Data tab for trends and badges.
  5. Add Home Screen or Lock Screen widgets to see what is expiring soon.

For a fuller walkthrough, open Settings → User Guide in the app.

3. Features

Home

Filter by In Use / Expiring Soon / Expired and by category. Tap an item to edit. Documents & contracts appear under Virtual Items. Swipe left to archive and choose an outcome.

Categories

Medicine, Food, Cosmetics, Daily Goods, Digital, Documents & Contracts, and Other. Each category has tailored date fields and default reminder rules (for example, cosmetics support period-after-opening; digital focuses on warranty; documents use validity dates).

Archive

History of finished items and outcomes (finished normally, expired & discarded, cleared / gifted, damaged, and more). Optional amounts feed waste / recovered totals. You can filter and search. Tapping a row does not open editing; long-press to Restore (back to Home) or Delete permanently.

Data

Insights from archive outcomes: processed / waste / recovered, monthly trends, category ranking, zero-waste challenges, and badges. “This year so far” is a live year-to-date summary. Your formal annual report for the previous calendar year unlocks on New Year’s Day (tap “View my report”, or open from the New Year reminder). Amounts only include optional ¥ you entered while archiving.

Barcode scanning (Pro)

Scan a package barcode to look up product details. Networked lookups may be limited: about 80 per year on Yearly Pro, about 160 per year on Lifetime Pro. If nothing is found, enter details manually or use photo recognition.

Photo recognition (Pro)

Photograph names, production dates, or shelf-life text to help fill the form. Recognition runs on your device—always verify before saving.

Local reminders

Local notifications fire before expiry. Enable Notifications in iOS Settings. Free users get built-in defaults; custom offsets require Pro. Archiving cancels pending alerts for that item.

Declutter Assistant

Settings → Declutter Assistant: a 0–100 health score based on expired and soon-due items, plus grouped lists to prioritize. When you have items in use, the score also appears beside the menu row (e.g. “72 pts”).

Widgets

Widgets show upcoming expiries, category lists, and high-risk medicine on the Lock Screen. Data is shared on-device via an App Group—no account required.

Backup (Pro)

Settings → Data Backup: export or import JSON / Excel (optional photos) for device migration. The Data tab itself does not export files.

4. Pro

The free tier includes manual entry, basic reminders, archive, data insights, badges, Declutter Assistant, and widgets. Pro unlocks:

  • Networked barcode product lookup (~80/year on Yearly; ~160/year on Lifetime)
  • Photo recognition
  • Custom notification / reminder settings
  • Backup export / import

Yearly subscription and lifetime purchase are available (prices shown in-app / App Store). Purchases and Restore Purchases use your Apple ID.

Restore Purchases only restores entitlements already bought with the current Apple ID. If you have never purchased, “nothing to restore” is expected.

5. Permissions

  • Camera — scan barcodes and capture labels or photos.
  • Photos — attach images from your library.
  • Notifications — deliver expiry reminders and the annual-report reminder.

You can revoke access anytime in Settings. Turning a permission off disables that feature only.

6. FAQ

I didn’t get today’s reminder

  • Confirm Notifications are allowed for the app.
  • Check that the category’s reminders are enabled and include day 0 (on the day).
  • If you edited the item after the scheduled morning time, a catch-up notification may arrive later.

Barcode lookup finds nothing

  • Many products are missing from public databases—enter details manually or use photo recognition.
  • If your lookup quota is used up, switch to manual / photo entry; barcodes you’ve saved before are reused when possible.

Photo recognition is inaccurate

Poor lighting, glare, or tiny print reduce accuracy. Shoot the date area clearly and verify before saving.

I can’t edit items in Archive

Archive is history-only. To change details: long-press → Restore to Home → edit → archive again if needed.

When is the annual report ready?

The formal report covers the previous calendar year and unlocks on January 1. With notifications enabled, you may also get a New Year reminder; the first open in January can present it once if last year had archives.

Widget looks stale

Open the main app once, then refresh the Home Screen. iOS refreshes widgets on its own schedule—not every second.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes on-device items and settings. If you exported backups, delete those files separately wherever you stored them.
